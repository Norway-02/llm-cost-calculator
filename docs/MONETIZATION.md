# Monetization & Affiliate Integration Strategy

## Monetization Framework

1. **Non-Intrusive Sponsored Cards**: Developer-facing sponsor slots (`components/AdSlot.tsx`) positioned below cost summaries.
2. **Target Categories**:
   - AI Infrastructure & Serverless GPUs (Together.ai, DeepInfra, RunPod, Lambda)
   - LLM Observability & Tracing (LangSmith, Helicone, Arize)
   - Vector Databases & RAG (Pinecone, Qdrant, Weaviate)
   - Cloud Hosting & Edge AI (Vercel, Cloudflare)
3. **Ad Placement Safety**: Ad slots are fixed-height and feature-flag controlled to prevent layout shift near input controls.
